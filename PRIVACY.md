## 📖 Vocabulary Learner Privacy

- **Account Authentication:** When signing in with Google, we only access your basic public profile identifier and email to associate your daily vocabulary streak.
- **Streak & Word Progress:** Your saved words and calendar streaks are stored in Firestore strictly under your authenticated UID.
- **Zero Advertising Trackers:** We do not sell or monetize student or learner vocabulary data with third-party advertisers.
- **Account Deletion:** You can delete your word history and sign out at any time.
